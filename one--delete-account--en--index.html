26/11/2026 · os.friday.one

Controller and contact

Friday One - Docs & Reminders (os.friday.one). Controller: Cristian Martinez, Spain. Privacy and support: fridayonedocs@gmail.com.

Local documents and copies

Deleting One data, unlinking One and deleting the global Friday ID are different operations. Removing a link must not erase unrelated records. A web request cannot remotely erase files on offline devices or copies you exported elsewhere. Support can explain how to remove local One data without affecting other Friday apps.

Request deletion of One data and its product link

Email fridayonedocs@gmail.com with the subject “One only — deletion request” and your @handle, if known. You do not need to reinstall the app. Specify that Fuel, Orbit and your global Friday ID must remain intact. This is a manually handled request concerning One-specific data, product linkage and access, not global identity deletion. Support verifies ownership proportionately without requesting recovery secrets, identifies the applicable records and explains scope, justified exceptions and the outcome. A request is not automatic erasure or proof of completion. Records necessary for other Friday services must not be removed as One-only data. Where a further authorised administrative operation is needed, support must complete it before confirming deletion.

Global Friday ID deletion

Global identity deletion requires a separate, explicit request and may affect other Friday apps. See the existing Friday ID deletion page. Do not send passwords, FRK2, review codes, private keys, purchase tokens or document contents to support.

Security, retention and your rights

Local stores and voluntarily exported backups use encryption. Keep your exported backup and password safe: recovering Friday ID does not retrieve missing documents. Local documents remain until you remove them; exported copies must be managed separately. Identity and session records are kept while necessary to provide the shared Friday ID service. Minimum evidence of an administrative right is separate from documents and retained while it supports that right. Security, abuse-prevention and request records require a specific purpose and must be erased or restricted when that need ends, unless an applicable legal obligation or claim requires retention. No unlimited retention is authorised. You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability where applicable, and withdraw consent where it is the basis of processing. Contact fridayonedocs@gmail.com. You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or another competent authority. Requests are answered without undue delay and generally within one month; a justified extension must be communicated within that month. Receipt of a request does not mean deletion has completed.
