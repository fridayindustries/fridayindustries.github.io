Privacy policy

26/11/2026 · os.friday.one

Controller and contact

Friday One - Docs & Reminders (os.friday.one). Controller: Cristian Martinez, Spain. Privacy and support: fridayonedocs@gmail.com.

Documents and local processing

Library, Agenda, Garage, scans, study plans, OCR, search and annotations are processed on the device. Documents and voluntarily exported encrypted backups are not stored or recovered by Friday Server. Guest Free does not require Friday ID or Friday Server. The bundled OCR model can work offline.

Friday ID and external services

Optional Friday ID manages identity, profile, devices, public keys, sessions and product-specific rights. It does not synchronize document contents. ML Kit can send installation identifiers, device/app information, performance and error metrics to Google; local OCR does not mean the SDK makes no communications. Support email is handled by Gmail. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. This static site adds no analytics, remote fonts or forms.

Purposes and legal bases

Documents are processed on your device to provide the functions you choose; their contents may include personal or sensitive information, so use only documents you are entitled to manage. Optional Friday ID authentication, sessions and rights are processed to provide the requested service. Necessary service security and abuse prevention are based on the legitimate interest in protecting accounts and the service, subject to applicable rights. Support handles the information you voluntarily send to answer your request and meet applicable obligations. Where consent is required, it must be specific to the purpose and can be withdrawn; an Android permission is not a blanket privacy consent. Google and GitHub processing follows their applicable notices; self-hosting Friday Server does not determine where those providers process data.

Security, retention and your rights

Local stores and voluntarily exported backups use encryption. Keep your exported backup and password safe: recovering Friday ID does not retrieve missing documents. Local documents remain until you remove them; exported copies must be managed separately. Identity and session records are kept while necessary to provide the shared Friday ID service. Minimum evidence of an administrative right is separate from documents and retained while it supports that right. Security, abuse-prevention and request records require a specific purpose and must be erased or restricted when that need ends, unless an applicable legal obligation or claim requires retention. No unlimited retention is authorised. You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability where applicable, and withdraw consent where it is the basis of processing. Contact fridayonedocs@gmail.com. You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or another competent authority. Requests are answered without undue delay and generally within one month; a justified extension must be communicated within that month. Receipt of a request does not mean deletion has completed.
