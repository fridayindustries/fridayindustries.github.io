Privacy

Fuel uses food entries, quantities, nutrients, recipes, photos and preferences; the optional profile may include age, height, weight, goals and activity. Their combinations may reveal health information. The diary is stored mainly on the device, separated by identity. Friday ID manages accounts, devices and sessions; private device keys stay in Android Keystore.

Fuel Vision / Google Play

Fuel Vision performs on-device inference after downloading the model. Optional remote assistance is separate: it may send labels and, if selected, photos to the server. Not all photos can be said to always stay on the phone. Friday ID, permissions and rights use service communications. ML Kit may send Google device and app information, installation identifiers, errors and usage and performance metrics; some features use Firebase Installations and Remote Config. Local recognition does not exclude these communications. This beta does not allow purchases or trial activation; these are not presented as operational. Support uses Gmail and receives your email address, message and minimum information needed to respond: do not send health information. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs IP addresses for security; this static site contains no forms, analytics or external resources. The location of the self-hosted server does not determine where Google or GitHub process data.

Your privacy requests

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability where applicable, and withdraw consent where it is the basis for processing. You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or another competent authority. The GDPR requires a response without undue delay and generally within one month; a justified extension must be communicated within that month. Receipt of a request does not establish that erasure has been completed.

1. Request Friday Fuel deletion

The request concerns Fuel only: its data, product link and access. It does not request deletion of Friday ID, Orbit or Friday AI. Support must check ownership and explain scope, justified exceptions and outcome. Sending email neither performs automatic erasure nor confirms completion.

2. Request global Friday ID deletion

This request covers your shared identity, devices, sessions and data associated with that identity across Friday products. It may remove access to Fuel, Orbit, Friday AI and other linked products. Support must explain the scope and verify ownership before deletion. A Fuel-only request must not be processed as a global request.

Data on your devices

A web request does not immediately erase diaries, photos, recipes or copies on offline devices. Support must explain how to export or remove local Fuel data and copies under your control without affecting other identities. A nutrition backup cannot recover Friday ID.

Data that may be retained

Local data remains until you remove it or an applicable erasure is carried out. Minimum purchase or Lifetime ownership evidence is separate from content and retained while it substantiates an active right. Abuse prevention, security and request records require a specific purpose and must be erased or restricted when that need ends, unless an applicable obligation or claim requires retention. Unlimited retention is not authorised. Backups and exports are handled separately; a request does not immediately remove disconnected copies.

Subscriptions and access

Fuel data deletion is separate from Friday ID deletion. Deleting Fuel data does not erase purchased rights: minimal proof is retained while a right remains valid so its owner can expressly restore it. Lifetime is not transferable and Google Play subscriptions are not cancelled by deletion. The free trial needs no card and has no automatic renewal; deletion does not restart a consumed trial. Purchases and trial activation are disabled in this beta.

Fuel beta: aged 18 or over

This first Friday Fuel closed beta is exclusively for people aged 18 or over. A birth date with no default value is requested before access. Only the admission decision is stored on the device, not the date. This is a declared-age screen, not documentary verification or permission to process health data. The beta does not admit minors or use the parental authorisation flow. This restriction applies to the Fuel beta and does not change other products using Friday ID. Permissions for nutrition processing, photo transmission or SDK use are explicitly chosen per purpose and are independent of Premium. The minimal server record contains Friday ID, declared country, purposes, notice version, state, revision and decision time. It contains no birth date or identity documents. New operations requiring an updated permission check are unavailable offline.

How to request deletion

Specify “Fuel only” or “Entire Friday ID” and your @handle, if known. Do not send FRK2, FRI, passwords, private keys, purchase tokens, personal photos or your diary. You do not need to reinstall Fuel to start a request. Support will use proportionate ownership verification without requesting recovery secrets.
