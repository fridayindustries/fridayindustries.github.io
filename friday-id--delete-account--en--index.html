How to request deletion

2. Request global Friday ID deletion

This request covers your shared identity, devices, sessions and data associated with that identity across Friday products. It may remove access to Fuel, Orbit, Friday AI and other linked products. Support must explain the scope and verify ownership before deletion. A Fuel-only request must not be processed as a global request. Receipt of a request does not confirm global deletion. Ownership must be checked and consequences for linked products explained before action; email does not provide automatic erasure.

Data on your devices

A web request does not immediately erase diaries, photos, recipes or copies on offline devices. Support must explain how to export or remove local Fuel data and copies under your control without affecting other identities. A nutrition backup cannot recover Friday ID.

Data that may be retained

Local data remains until you remove it or an applicable erasure is carried out. Minimum purchase or Lifetime ownership evidence is separate from content and retained while it substantiates an active right. Abuse prevention, security and request records require a specific purpose and must be erased or restricted when that need ends, unless an applicable obligation or claim requires retention. Unlimited retention is not authorised. Backups and exports are handled separately; a request does not immediately remove disconnected copies.

Subscriptions and access

Fuel data deletion is separate from Friday ID deletion. Deleting Fuel data does not erase purchased rights: minimal proof is retained while a right remains valid so its owner can expressly restore it. Lifetime is not transferable and Google Play subscriptions are not cancelled by deletion. The free trial needs no card and has no automatic renewal; deletion does not restart a consumed trial. Purchases and trial activation are disabled in this beta.

How to request deletion

Specify “Fuel only” or “Entire Friday ID” and your @handle, if known. Do not send FRK2, FRI, passwords, private keys, purchase tokens, personal photos or your diary. You do not need to reinstall Fuel to start a request. Support will use proportionate ownership verification without requesting recovery secrets.
